Realistic resus scenarios on any screen.

Drive live vitals, cardiac rhythms and ventilator waveforms from one device; project the patient monitor on another. Built for emergency and critical-care simulation.

New in v2 — Physiology mode. Set the patient's blood volume, vascular tone, contractility and lungs; a physiology engine works out every number and waveform, with heart–lung interaction and gas exchange. Manual mode still works exactly as before.

For training and education only. ResusSim is not a medical device and must never be used for real patient care, diagnosis, or treatment decisions. All waveforms and values are simulated; all patient details are fictional.

Scenario script
Clear
Prepare a scenario that runs on its own. Steps fire in order: each waits for its time or trigger once the step before it has fired, then acts like a button press — it is logged in Activity and the debrief (the trainee console doesn’t show it). An any time step doesn’t wait its turn: it is armed from Start and fires once, whenever its condition holds, until the steps in order have all fired. The script runs on this Controller only and keeps the patient’s time, so it stops while you are on another screen.

Resuscitation
Compressions, shocks and pacing act on the simulated heart. Sustained hypoxaemia or profound shock can arrest the patient on their own; good CPR with adrenaline repays the heart’s oxygen debt, so shocks work more often and a treated PEA can return a circulation.
